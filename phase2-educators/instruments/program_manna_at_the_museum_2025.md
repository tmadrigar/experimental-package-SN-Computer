# Phase 2: "Manna at the Museum 2025" (Manna-Teacher Model)

Immersive teacher training held on 14 to 16 March 2025 at the Museu Oscar Niemeyer, Curitiba, Paraná,
Brazil (about 33 hours). Participants were selected through a national public call (Edital nº 12/2025)
open to public school teachers of all levels of basic education and to public university faculty.
235 educators were credentialed; 87 completed both the pre-test and the post-test.

## Activities by phase of the framework

| Phase | Activities |
|---|---|
| Conception | Program built on three strands: exponential technologies (Artificial Intelligence and Quantum Computing), innovation in the classroom, and educators' well-being and soft skills |
| Diagnostic assessment | Profile and expectations questionnaire; 10-item multiple-choice pre-test on exponential technologies (baseline for the normalized gain) |
| Formative phase | Minicourses and panels (e.g., "Education 5.0", "Quantum Computing: The Next Revolution", "The Power of Quantum: From the Invisible to the Impossible"); hands-on stations (Manna Experience) with AI, Quantum Computing, Internet of Drones, robotics, and games; well-being and soft-skills dynamics; **Manna VAI** ("Let's Learn Innovation"), a team dynamic in which educators designed a proposal combining exponential technologies and innovation for their own classroom; spontaneous comments recorded in the myMobiConf app |
| Summative phase | **Manna Show**: teams presented their Manna VAI proposals as short pitches, and the most disruptive ideas were highlighted collectively; **Manna Spaces** workshop ("Exponentials: Planning the Future") on applying the methodology in each school; post-test; institutional replication commitment forms |

## Instruments
- **Pre-test and post-test:** 10 multiple-choice questions on exponential technologies, scored 0 to 10.
  Item-level results (1 = correct, 0 = incorrect): `phase2-educators/data/pre_post_test_87_educators.csv`.
- **Spontaneous comments:** open text recorded in the app during the event and analyzed through
  sentiment analysis (Google Cloud Natural Language API, followed by manual review) and thematic
  analysis. The texts may identify participants and are not published.
- **Replication commitment form:** signed by the participants at the end of the event
  (103 schools in 17 states and the Federal District). Individual forms contain personal and
  institutional contact data and are not published.
