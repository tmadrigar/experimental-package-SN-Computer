# Phase 1: curriculum and summative assessment

## IoD Bootcamp "Férias das Feras" (January 2024)

Five days, four hours per day, in Maringá, Campo Mourão, Cianorte, and Paranavaí (Paraná, Brazil).
The same instructors and the same ten-module curriculum were used for experimental and control groups;
the only difference was the criterion used to form the teams.

| Module | Topic |
|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction to the Internet of Drones |
| 2 | Evolution of the Internet and the Era of Drones |
| 3 | Building a Joystick with Arduino |
| 4 | Classification and Applications of Drones |
| 5 | Essential Components of a Drone |
| 6 | Programming and Automation of Drones |
| 7 | Flight Modes and Drone Control |
| 8 | Ethics and Responsibility in Drone Use |
| 9 | Piloting Practice |
| 10 | Presentation and Evaluation of Projects |

**Formative assessment:** at the end of each of the nine classes, five objective questions were
administered on the Kahoot platform (45 items per student). Data: `phase1-students/data/`.

## Innovation Competition rubric (summative assessment)

Teams proposed IoD solutions to real-world problems and presented them in 2-minute pitches to an
evaluation panel that did not know whether each team was experimental or control. Each criterion
was scored on a 1 to 10 scale.

| Criterion | Description | Weight (%) |
|---|---|---|
| Originality and Innovation | Creativity and novelty of the proposed solution relative to existing approaches | 25 |
| Practical Applicability | Feasibility and applicability of the solution in a real-world context | 20 |
| Technical Complexity | Depth of technical implementation and appropriate use of Internet of Drones technologies | 15 |
| Teamwork | Effectiveness of collaboration and role distribution among team members | 25 |
| Presentation | Clarity, organization, and persuasiveness of the solution defense | 15 |
