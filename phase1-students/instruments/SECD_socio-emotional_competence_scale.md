# SECD: Socio-Emotional Competence Scale

25-item self-report scale developed by the Manna Team for the IoD Bootcamps, inspired by the
Social Emotional Competence Questionnaire (SECQ; Zhou & Ee, 2012) and informed by the CASEL
framework. It covers four of the five CASEL competency areas (self-awareness, self-management,
social awareness, and relationship skills); responsible decision-making is not measured.

**Response scale (5-point Likert):** 1 = Never, 2 = Rarely, 3 = Sometimes, 4 = Often, 5 = Always.
**Scoring:** the score of each dimension is the sum of its five items (5 to 25); the total score is the sum of all items (25 to 125).
**Use in the study:** diagnostic input, together with Kolb's Learning Style Inventory (LSI), for profile-based team formation (Phase 1).

**Note on item 9.** In the version applied in the bootcamps, item 9 (Emotional Regulation) has the
same wording as item 5 (Emotional Awareness). The scale is reproduced here exactly as it was applied,
and the item is kept in both dimensions. In future versions of the SECD, item 9 will be replaced by an
item specific to emotional regulation, as part of the psychometric validation of the scale.

Kolb's Learning Style Inventory (LSI) is a published instrument and is not reproduced here; see Kolb & Kolb (2005).
